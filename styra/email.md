**Project:** Styra AI Fashion Assistant  
**Target Markets:** Portugal and Bangladesh

## Subject
Styra AI Fashion Assistant — Project Scope and Next Steps

## Email

Hi,

I’m sharing an initial overview of Styra, an AI-powered fashion shopping assistant designed to help users discover clothing that suits their personal style, preferences, and shopping needs.

The project will explore how an AI-assisted experience can make fashion discovery more convenient and personalized, with an initial focus on users in Portugal and Bangladesh.

### Initial project objectives

- Understand users’ fashion shopping behaviours, needs, and frustrations.
- Explore opportunities for AI-assisted product discovery and personalized recommendations.
- Develop a user-centred experience that makes browsing and choosing fashion products easier.
- Consider the needs and expectations of users in both target markets.

### Proposed scope

The initial design process will include:

- Project brief and problem definition
- User research and competitor analysis
- User personas and journey mapping
- User flows and storyboard development
- Wireframes and interface design
- Prototyping and usability evaluation

### Points requiring further clarification

Before finalizing the scope, I would like to clarify the following:

- Which user group should be prioritized in the initial release?
- Which fashion shopping problems should the product address first?
- What role should AI play in product discovery and recommendations?
- What are the key success metrics for evaluating the experience?

Once these points are clarified, the project scope and priorities can be refined accordingly.
